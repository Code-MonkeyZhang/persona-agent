## What Is an Agent App

An Agent App is an extension with its own interface. While ordinary tools only return text results, an Agent App provides a real operating surface the agent can drive from conversations, and that you can open and use yourself.

### What It Looks Like

Installed Agent Apps appear in the phone panel on the right side of the app. The panel is a portrait screen with a home, a clock, and a grid of app icons, shaped like a small phone embedded in your desktop.

### What It Can Do

- The agent operates the app from a conversation, for example writing a task into a todo app.
- The app can push notifications to the agent that trigger it to talk to you, like a reminder app calling you on time.
- You can also open the app directly in the phone panel and use it manually, no conversation required.

### Relation to Skills and Tools

The three are parallel extension forms. Skills are knowledge, tools are actions, apps are services with interfaces. A well-rounded character often combines all three, see [Overview](/docs/getting-started).

When ready, move on to [Install and Use](/docs/agent-apps).

## Install and Use

### Installing

Click the compass at the bottom of the agent bar to enter the marketplace, switch to the apps tab, search or browse, and click install. The app downloads and registers locally, and its icon appears on the phone panel home immediately. The pomodoro timer is a typical Agent App example.

![Pomodoro Timer](/docs-images/app-pomodoro.png)

### Opening and Closing the Phone Panel

The phone panel is collapsed by default. Click the toggle on the right side of the title bar to expand it, click again to collapse, and drag to adjust its width.

### Using Apps

Tap an app icon on the phone panel home to open it. The app runs with its full interface, the top bar provides back navigation, and Esc steps back level by level.

- Manual use: operate the interface directly.
- Conversational use: simply tell the agent to do it, like asking it to note a task, and it drives the app for you.

For personalizing the layout after installing several apps, see [Layout and Wallpaper](/docs/agent-apps).

## Layout and Wallpaper

### Rearranging Apps

The phone panel home supports drag and drop. Hold an icon, drag it to the desired spot, and release. Put frequent apps up front and occasional ones behind, the layout is entirely yours. If things get messy, the phone panel settings offer a one-click layout reset.

### Changing the Wallpaper

Open the built-in settings app on the phone panel and go to the wallpaper page. Pick a built-in wallpaper or upload an image of your own.

### Preferences Follow You

App arrangement and wallpaper choice are stored on the server side, so the layout follows automatically when another device connects to the same host, no re-arranging needed.
