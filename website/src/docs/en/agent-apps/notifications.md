Agent Apps do not just wait to be invoked, they can reach out to you at the right moment.

## How a Notification Happens

The app sends a notification through its own channel, the application validates it and turns it into a message injected into the agent's conversation, and the agent then speaks to you about the event. The whole chain stays on your machine.

## Typical Scenarios

- A reminder app hits the scheduled time and the agent speaks up.
- A monitoring app notices a change and the agent reports proactively.
- A task app finishes background work and the agent notifies you to check.

## When You Will Receive Them

Only installed and connected apps send notifications. To stop hearing from one, uninstall it in settings, see [Install and Use](/docs/agent-apps/install).
