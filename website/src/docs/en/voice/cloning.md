---
title: Voice Cloning
description: Clone a voice of your own from a recording
---

Beyond preset voices, you can upload a recording and clone a voice exclusively for your characters.

## Recording Requirements

- Ten seconds to five minutes long, richer content yields better results.
- mp3, m4a, or wav, no larger than twenty megabytes.
- Record in a quiet environment at a steady pace, background noise hurts cloning quality.

## Creating a Cloned Voice

Open the voice service tab in settings, click clone new voice in the clone management card, fill in a name and submit the audio file. The voice appears in the list when done and supports preview, rename, and delete.

## Binding to a Character

Once created, the cloned voice shows up in the voice card of the agent editor. It works exactly like a preset, see [Preset Voices](/docs/voice/presets).
