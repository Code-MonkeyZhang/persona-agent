title: Preset Voices
description: Choose from fifty plus preset voices for your character
---

The app bundles more than fifty preset voices covering different genders, styles, and language tendencies, ready out of the box.

## Binding a Voice to a Character

- Open the character's [editor](/docs/agents/create) and go to the voice card.
- Preview and pick a voice from the list.
- Set the narration language for this character, synthesis follows it during playback.

## One Voice per Character

Voice is a property of the character, not a global setting. You can give each character a different voice, and it follows automatically when you switch agents.

## Preview Tip

The same voice performs differently under different TTS models. After switching the [synthesis model](/docs/voice/setup), preview the voice again.

If presets do not satisfy you, craft one from your own recording in [Voice Cloning](/docs/voice/cloning).
