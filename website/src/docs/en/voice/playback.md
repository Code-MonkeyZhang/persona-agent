title: Voice Playback
description: Turn on voice playback and the auto summary for long replies
---

With the voice service configured, characters can read their replies aloud.

## Enabling Playback

The chat header has a voice toggle. Once on, replies in the current session are synthesized and played asynchronously, text and voice arrive in parallel without blocking each other.

## Summarized Playback for Long Replies

Some replies are too long to enjoy verbatim. A summary threshold in voice settings has long replies distilled into key points before speaking instead of being read word by word. The default is two hundred characters, tune it to your taste.

- Raise it a lot to hear the full original text.
- Keep the default or lower it for brief playback.

## Mute and Restore

Click the voice toggle in the chat header at any time to mute, text replies are unaffected, and turning it back on resumes playback.

For choosing and changing voices see [Preset Voices](/docs/voice/presets) and [Voice Cloning](/docs/voice/cloning).
